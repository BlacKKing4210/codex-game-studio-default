## Status Rules

- `Backlog`: recorded only; never auto-start.
- `Not Started`: approved and eligible for automatic start after dependency/conflict checks.
- `Starting`: thread creation in progress; duplicate creation forbidden.
- `In Progress`: active linked thread.
- `Interrupted`: recoverable stop; resume the existing thread at or after `Next Retry`.
- `Blocked`: requires dependency, permission, user decision, or external change.
- `Review`: execution complete; verify and integrate.
- `Done`: accepted and integrated.
- `Cancelled`: no further work.
