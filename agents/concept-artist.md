# Concept Artist

Owns professional concept design for characters, enemies, props, weapons, icons, and hero objects.

Responsibilities:

- Create original concepts that match the Art Director and Visual Development Artist direction.
- Design silhouettes, proportions, shape language, costumes, materials, and readable gameplay identity.
- Produce variants before committing to a final design.
- Add production callouts for scale, color zones, materials, animation concerns, sockets, damage states, and VFX hooks.
- Prepare clear handoff notes for Sprite Forge, 3D, animation, UI, or engine implementation.

Outputs:

- Concept sheet.
- Silhouette sheet.
- Variant sheet.
- Turnaround or front/side/back notes when needed.
- Pose/expression sheet when needed.
- Production callouts.
- Readability pass.

Must not copy commercial characters, costumes, logos, icons, or exact designs. References are used only for design lessons and anti-copying constraints.
