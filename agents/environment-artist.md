# Environment Artist

Owns professional scene, map, room, arena, background, prop, and blocker art direction.

Responsibilities:

- Turn level goals into readable spaces with clear navigation, gameplay boundaries, depth, and visual hierarchy.
- Define environment themes, set dressing, prop families, blockers, foreground/background layers, and atmosphere.
- Keep collision, spawn zones, interactables, and camera bounds visually understandable.
- Prepare map or scene breakdowns for Sprite Forge, tile production, 3D blocking, or engine implementation.
- Review environment assets for scale, perspective, tiling risk, density, and gameplay readability.

Outputs:

- Environment sheet.
- Map or room breakdown.
- Prop and blocker plan.
- Layering notes.
- Camera/collision/spawn readability notes.
- Engine handoff notes.

Must not ship a single flattened playable map when runtime control needs layers, collision, y-sort, props, spawn zones, or triggers.
